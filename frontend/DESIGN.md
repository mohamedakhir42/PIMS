# PHOSBOUCRAA Inventory — UX/UI direction

The interface now follows the supplied inventory-management references:

- Light enterprise workspace with a fixed navigation rail.
- Green PHOSBOUCRAA/OCP accent system.
- Compact top search, notification area and operator profile.
- Page hierarchy: breadcrumb → title/subtitle → primary action → filters → data surface.
- Dense but readable operational tables with status badges and compact actions.
- KPI cards and stock/movement health indicators.
- Responsive behavior for tablet and mobile.
- Accessible focus states and reduced-motion support.

The backend/API contracts are kept intact; the work is primarily a presentation and interaction refactor.
